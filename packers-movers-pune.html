Planning checklist

List belongings, flag fragile items, confirm furniture disassembly requirements, and keep essential documents with you. Before moving day, reconfirm the address, pickup window, destination access, payment terms, and contact person.

Insurance and documentation

Ask what insurance is offered, covered risks, exclusions, and how to raise a claim. Keep the signed estimate, inventory, receipts, and condition photos.
